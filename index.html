blackbabata Studio

A personal publishing tool for the blackbabata channel.

What this is

blackbabata publishes short English-vocabulary videos, one word at a time, in two series: an elementary-school series and a TOEIC business-vocabulary series. Each new word is followed by review videos scheduled on a spaced-repetition curve, so viewers meet each word again just before they would forget it.

blackbabata Studio is the small tool the channel owner uses to upload those videos to the channel's own TikTok account, @blackbabata. It connects to TikTok through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

What this is not

  • It is not a public service and has no sign-up. It does not create accounts for visitors.
  • It does not collect, store or process any data about the people who watch the videos or visit this site.
  • It only ever posts videos made by the channel owner, to the channel owner's own account.
